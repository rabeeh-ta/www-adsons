# Adsons owner content checklist

Use this checklist during the owner review. Do not publish unverified details.

## Company identity

- [ ] Confirm full registered and trading company name
- [ ] Supply official logo in SVG or high-resolution PNG
- [ ] Confirm year established and wording for the 20+ year claim
- [ ] Confirm headquarters or principal business location
- [ ] Confirm whether Adsons owns branded products, trades third-party products, or both
- [ ] Confirm whether private-label supply is offered
- [ ] Approve the company story and any founder/family history

## Trading

- [ ] Confirm priority buyer types
- [ ] Confirm Africa, Asia and North America as public regions
- [ ] Add any other regions that are safe to mention
- [ ] Approve statements about sourcing, distribution and trading capabilities
- [ ] Confirm safe quality, logistics or fulfilment claims
- [ ] Decide whether any country names may be shown publicly

## Categories

- [ ] Review the six draft category groups
- [ ] Add, remove or rename broad categories
- [ ] Review every public subcategory for competitive sensitivity
- [ ] Approve one short description per category
- [ ] Confirm that no product, brand, supplier, price, stock or catalogue details appear

## Retail network

- [ ] Official name of each shop
- [ ] Full address and map link for each shop
- [ ] Opening days and hours
- [ ] Store contact number
- [ ] Accessibility or parking information, if useful
- [ ] Exterior and interior photographs for each shop

## Contact

- [ ] Official WhatsApp number with international country code
- [ ] Main business phone number
- [ ] Public email address
- [ ] Confirm preferred WhatsApp message wording
- [ ] Confirm who receives wholesale, retail and partnership enquiries

## Photography and proof

- [ ] Current team or leadership photographs, if they should be public
- [ ] Office, warehouse or operational photography, if applicable
- [ ] Permission for any customer, supplier or partner logo
- [ ] Approved testimonials with name, role and company permission
- [ ] Verified statistics beyond 20+ years, three shops and three regions

## Legal and launch

- [ ] Registered company details for the footer
- [ ] Privacy notice, if analytics or enquiry data collection is added
- [ ] Cookie requirements based on final analytics setup
- [ ] Domain name and hosting ownership
- [ ] Final proofreading and owner approval
